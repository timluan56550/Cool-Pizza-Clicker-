What is it?
A small clicker game where you eat pizzas!

How to Play.

Click the Pizza - every 8 clicks you form a new pizza and gain a small bonus.

Open the Upgrade shop! - by clicking the notepad on your right side you can open upgrades, you unlock more upgrades by buying the previous upgrades!

Open the Rebirth shop! - by clicking the button at the bottom fo the notepad, you can switch between the two pages. Once you get enough to rebirth, click the rebirth button to get permanent bonuses, you will also get Pizza Coins to buy stuff from the rebirth shop!

Some features include -

    Critical hits - Get a chance to get more pizzas a click!

    Stack combos - click the target to stack your combo, gain higher multiplier, don't miss or it will reset.

    Lock combo - Click the Lock button to freeze and unfreeze your combo streak! That way you can click the pizza with the multiplier, and not lose your streak!

    Rebirth - Click the Rebirth button to Rebirth and get permanent bonuses! Get Pizza Coins to unlock more Upgrades

    Automation - Inside the Pizza Coin/Rebirth shop, buy automation upgrades like auto clickers or auto buyers!

    Try to reach a goal of 1 Trillion Pizzas!
